See EduPath in Action

A look at the real screens, filled with sample data. Register to use them with your own transcript.

STUDENT VIEW

All-in-one Dashboard

Your CGPA, credits completed and GPA progression in one view, alongside your standing in your cohort. Download the whole thing as a PDF report, or share it with an advisor through a link that expires on its own.

STUDENT VIEW

Target CGPA + AI Planner

Set the CGPA you are aiming for and how many credits you have left. EduPath works out the GPA you need, with best, realistic and worst-case outcomes so the target is honest rather than hopeful. The AI Planner then turns it into a semester-by-semester GPA plan, with guidance based on your own performance trend.

STUDENT VIEW

What-If Simulator

Try a grade before you earn it. Add a course you plan to retake or have not taken yet, pick a hypothetical grade, and see exactly how your CGPA moves. Two plans can be saved and compared side by side.

STUDENT VIEW

Grade Tracker

Enter coursework marks as they come back, as 8/10, 75, 68% or a letter grade. EduPath shows how you are doing so far, the grade you are heading for, and the approximate mark you still need in what is left to reach your target.

STUDENT VIEW

Peer Benchmarking

See where your semester GPA sits within your cohort, next to the department average. Everything is anonymous and combined: no names, no individual results, and nothing is shown at all when the group is too small.

STUDENT VIEW

Course Statistics

Average grade, fail rate and retake rate for each course, drawn from students who agreed to share. Know which courses your seniors found hard before you register for them.

STUDENT VIEW

Career Recommendation

Your transcript becomes a skill profile built on O*NET occupational data, then matched against the careers UM lists for your programme. Every match comes with the skills that explain it, not just a number.

STUDENT VIEW

EduPath AI Assistant

Ask about your own results in plain language: why your CGPA changed, whether you are on track for your target, or why a career was recommended. Answers are based on your transcript, and the assistant is one click away on every page.

STAFF VIEW

Platform Overview

Registered and verified users, uploaded transcripts and data-sharing choices at a glance, broken down by faculty and by programme.

STAFF VIEW

Academic Performance Trends

How many students are improving, stable, volatile or declining, filtered by programme and batch, so support can be aimed where it is needed. Counts only, and any group smaller than five is withheld.

STAFF VIEW

Course Difficulty

Grade spread, fail rate and retake rate for every course, ranked so the hardest surface first. A course only appears once enough students have taken it for no individual grade to be identifiable.
