About EduPath

Automated Transcript Parsing and Predictive Analytics System

Introduction

EduPath is a web-based academic performance analysis system developed to help students monitor, analyse, and predict their academic performance through automated transcript processing and data visualization features.

System Objectives

Performance Analysis & Prediction

Analyse GPA and CGPA trends across semesters, and predict what is needed to reach a target CGPA.

Academic Insights

Give students timely insight into their progress, both during the semester and compared with their peers, to support academic planning.

Career Recommendation

Recommend suitable career pathways based on academic results.

Main Features

Transcript Upload
Performance Analysis
Target CGPA Prediction
What-If Simulator
AI GPA Target Planner
Grade Tracker
Peer Benchmarking
Course Statistics
Career Recommendation
Dashboard & Report Sharing
EduPath AI Assistant
User Profile Management

Our Mission

EduPath aims to provide students with a smarter and more interactive platform for understanding academic progress, supporting academic planning, and planning future career pathways.
