How are these scenarios calculated?

Best Case — Assumes a 4.00 GPA for all remaining credits.

Realistic Case — Assumes your latest GPA is maintained.

Estimated Worst Case — Uses your historical semester-to-semester GPA changes to estimate volatility and considers your overall academic trend. The estimated decline is bounded to prevent unusually large changes from producing unrealistic results. This is a statistical estimate for planning purposes, not a guaranteed outcome.
