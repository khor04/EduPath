Privacy Policy

Your privacy and academic information are important to us.

Introduction

EduPath is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of users. This Privacy Policy explains how user data is collected, used, and protected within the system.

Information Collected

  • Username and email address
  • Academic transcript information
  • GPA and CGPA results
  • Profile pictures uploaded by users
  • Account login credentials
  • Name, Matric Number, and NRIC/Passport Number in transcript will not be collected

Use of Information

  • Academic performance analysis
  • GPA and CGPA visualization
  • Anonymous peer benchmarking
  • Career recommendation generation
  • User account management
  • System improvement and maintenance

Data Security

  • User passwords are encrypted before storage
  • Personal information is securely stored
  • Only authorized users can access their own records, do not share your password with others

Data Sharing

EduPath does not sell, trade, or share user personal information with third parties unless required by law.

Policy Updates

EduPath reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
