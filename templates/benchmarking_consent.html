Share Your Results Anonymously

If you choose to share, your academic results are combined with those of other students who also agreed to produce anonymous statistics:

  • Peer Benchmarking — compares your GPA with students in your programme and batch.
  • Course Statistics — course averages, fail rates and retake rates, shown to students.

Only combined figures are shown — never your name or individual grades. Statistics are hidden when there are too few students to protect anonymity.

Your choice controls whether your results are included in these statistics and whether you can view peer benchmarking and course statistics. You can change your choice anytime in Profile.
